## My read

This is a classic debt-consolidation cash-out. The Thompsons have strong income ($17,250/mo)
and significant equity ($249K), but they're drowning in high-interest credit card debt.
The refinance at 6.5% is a no-brainer when the alternative is 22% APR on $78K of cards.

The analytical crux: the three credit card accounts ($2,340/mo total) MUST be excluded
from DTI because they're being paid off at closing from loan proceeds. Without this
exclusion, back-end DTI is 37.3% and the file fails conventional. With exclusion, it's
a clean 23.7%.

Other notes: FICO 698 is borderline for best conventional pricing but acceptable. The
score is artificially depressed by high utilization and should improve post-consolidation.
The 80% LTV is exactly at the conventional cash-out maximum - no room for error. Seasoning
is well within requirements (5+ years owned). Perfect mortgage payment history.
