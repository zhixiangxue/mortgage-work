## My read

Solid conventional investment purchase. Thomas is a W-2 borrower with stable Delta
employment, clean credit, and adequate reserves for two investment properties. The
7.0% rate is standard for investment property (50-75 bps premium over primary
residence conventional). The key analytical challenge is the DTI: the file only
passes because 75% of subject property market rent is included in qualifying income
($1,425/mo). Without that offset, back-end DTI jumps to 37.9% and the file fails
conventional guidelines. Recommend conventional investment product, DU submission.

## Open questions

- The subject property market rent estimate ($1,900/mo) is from the property disclosure.
  Appraisal will confirm - if appraisal comes in lower, qualifying income drops and DTI
  may exceed 36%.
- The existing Schedule E shows $800/mo net. Fannie guidelines allow adding back
  depreciation. If depreciation is added back, qualifying rental income increases.
  Need full tax returns to verify depreciation amount.
- Borrower is buying as individual, not LLC. Some lenders prefer entity ownership for
  investment properties but Fannie allows individual borrower on conventional investment.
